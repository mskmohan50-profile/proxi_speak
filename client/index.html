❓ How to use ProxiSpeak
  • Move: W A S D / arrow keys, or click anywhere on the radar.
  • Talk: click Enable Mic, then speak (auto voice detection) or hold Space.
  • Two-person test: open this page in a second tab or on another device on your network (http://YOUR-IP:3000), use headphones, and walk apart: voices get quieter, pan left/right and muffle inside zones.
  • Chat: Enter to type; tick Everyone to broadcast to all.
  • Mute a person: use the 🔇 button on their card in the audible list (only affects you).
  • Zones: Auditorium = reverb, Library = muffled, Stage = amplified, Pod = private.
